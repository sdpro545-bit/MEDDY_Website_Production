* **Shadow Dynamics:** Sharp quay and crane leg drop shadows.
* **Depth of Field & Sharpness:** Deep terminal depth of field from foreground vessel to background gantry cranes.

## 2. Subjects & Uniform Compliance
* **Human Representation:** Terminal operations scale (no direct close-up subjects).
* **Uniform & PPE Standard:** N/A.

## 3. Brand Protection & Sanitization
* **Third-Party Marks Eliminated:** Removed 'Northern Justice' vessel text and 'SSA Terminals' crane markings.
* **Official Branding Placements:** Foreground vessel hull corporate fleet branding; crane cab identification panels; branded MEDDY blue containers.

## 4. Safe Zone Validation
Primary corporate elements are positioned strictly within the safe viewport band (`y = 22%–58%`, `x = 8%–92%`), ensuring complete visibility without truncation when cropped by service cards or header gradients.
