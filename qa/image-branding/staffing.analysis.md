# Visual & Retouching Analysis: staffing.webp

**Target Dimensions:** `900x1200 (3:4 aspect ratio)`  
**Site Crop Mode:** `3:4 service card; workers and back patch centered within safe zone.`

---

## 1. Photographic Properties
* **Camera Angle & Optics:** High-angle industrial construction deck shot, ~40mm lens.
* **Lighting Environment:** Bright overhead outdoor daylight with crisp sunlight on concrete slab.
* **Shadow Dynamics:** Strong cast shadows from workers and equipment onto rebar deck.
* **Depth of Field & Sharpness:** Deep aerial focus across operational slab.

## 2. Subjects & Uniform Compliance
* **Human Representation:** Nigerian engineering field crew, natural skin tones and proportions.
* **Uniform & PPE Standard:** Brand-blue coveralls with silver reflective tape; white hard hats with brand-blue crown stripe.

## 3. Brand Protection & Sanitization
* **Third-Party Marks Eliminated:** Removed all non-brand orange vests and foreign logos.
* **Official Branding Placements:** Standing technician large back corporate patch below collar; white hard hat logo.

## 4. Safe Zone Validation
Primary corporate elements are positioned strictly within the safe viewport band (`y = 22%–58%`, `x = 8%–92%`), ensuring complete visibility without truncation when cropped by service cards or header gradients.
