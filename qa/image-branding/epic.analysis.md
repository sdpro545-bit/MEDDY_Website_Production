# Visual & Retouching Analysis: epic.webp

**Target Dimensions:** `900x1200 (3:4 aspect ratio)`  
**Site Crop Mode:** `3:4 service card; primary drawing and helmet stay within 22%-58% vertical safe zone.`

---

## 1. Photographic Properties
* **Camera Angle & Optics:** Over-the-shoulder engineering control desk angle, ~50mm lens.
* **Lighting Environment:** Dual-monitor display glow combined with cool overhead technical office lighting.
* **Shadow Dynamics:** Soft contact shadows along desk surface, monitor stands, and engineer's collar.
* **Depth of Field & Sharpness:** Focused on right monitor displaying plant engineering diagram; soft foreground shoulder.

## 2. Subjects & Uniform Compliance
* **Human Representation:** Nigerian project engineer (back of head, neck, natural hair), deep brown skin tones.
* **Uniform & PPE Standard:** Charcoal engineering polo with embroidered emblem; branded white hard hat resting on desk.

## 3. Brand Protection & Sanitization
* **Third-Party Marks Eliminated:** Removed third-party monitor bezel branding and generic IDE source code.
* **Official Branding Placements:** Embroidered shoulder emblem; engineering drawing title block on monitor with logo and corporate title; branded desk helmet.

## 4. Safe Zone Validation
Primary corporate elements are positioned strictly within the safe viewport band (`y = 22%–58%`, `x = 8%–92%`), ensuring complete visibility without truncation when cropped by service cards or header gradients.
