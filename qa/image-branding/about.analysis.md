# Visual & Retouching Analysis: about.webp

**Target Dimensions:** `960x1200 (4:5 aspect ratio)`  
**Site Crop Mode:** `Used on About page as 4:5 card and homepage about preview. Primary branding kept within y=22%-58% safe zone.`

---

## 1. Photographic Properties
* **Camera Angle & Optics:** Eye-level medium-long shot, ~50mm focal length, natural perspective.
* **Lighting Environment:** Industrial ambient factory interior lighting with diffused top-down fluorescents and warm mechanical specular reflections.
* **Shadow Dynamics:** Soft ground falloff, natural occlusion under workwear folds and turbine casing.
* **Depth of Field & Sharpness:** Moderate depth of field, sharp foreground technicians with soft mechanical background.

## 2. Subjects & Uniform Compliance
* **Human Representation:** Two Nigerian engineering specialists (left worker profile, right worker three-quarters rear), natural melanin skin tones, realistic pores and knuckles.
* **Uniform & PPE Standard:** Navy cotton-drill workwear with charcoal accents, two silver retro-reflective bands on sleeves/legs, white hard hats with brand-blue stripe and official logo.

## 3. Brand Protection & Sanitization
* **Third-Party Marks Eliminated:** Eliminated all non-brand yellow hi-vis vests and foreign turbine tags.
* **Official Branding Placements:** Left helmet centered logo; left chest white embroidered patch with full corporate title; right technician back corporate patch; plain dark diagnostic tablet.

## 4. Safe Zone Validation
Primary corporate elements are positioned strictly within the safe viewport band (`y = 22%–58%`, `x = 8%–92%`), ensuring complete visibility without truncation when cropped by service cards or header gradients.
