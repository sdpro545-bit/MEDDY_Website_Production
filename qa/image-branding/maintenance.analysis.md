# Visual & Retouching Analysis: maintenance.webp

**Target Dimensions:** `900x1200 (3:4 aspect ratio)`  
**Site Crop Mode:** `3:4 service card; pump tag centered in active brand window.`

---

## 1. Photographic Properties
* **Camera Angle & Optics:** Close-up mechanical intervention angle, ~70mm lens.
* **Lighting Environment:** Industrial overhead inspection lighting with warm highlights on metal pump volute.
* **Shadow Dynamics:** Complex cast shadows from wrench, bolts, and technician hands.
* **Depth of Field & Sharpness:** Shallow depth of field focused on wrench contact point on pump casing.

## 2. Subjects & Uniform Compliance
* **Human Representation:** Nigerian mechanical technician (hands and forearms), deep brown skin with realistic pores.
* **Uniform & PPE Standard:** Blue cotton-drill coverall sleeve with silver reflective band; charcoal glove with blue cuff.

## 3. Brand Protection & Sanitization
* **Third-Party Marks Eliminated:** Eliminated foreign pump casting brand stamps.
* **Official Branding Placements:** Stainless steel 'Serviced by' tag riveted to pump casing with logo and full corporate name; sleeve patch.

## 4. Safe Zone Validation
Primary corporate elements are positioned strictly within the safe viewport band (`y = 22%–58%`, `x = 8%–92%`), ensuring complete visibility without truncation when cropped by service cards or header gradients.
