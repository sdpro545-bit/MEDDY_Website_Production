# Visual & Retouching Analysis: marine.webp

**Target Dimensions:** `900x1200 (3:4 aspect ratio)`  
**Site Crop Mode:** `3:4 service card; superstructure branding perfectly framed in primary zone.`

---

## 1. Photographic Properties
* **Camera Angle & Optics:** Low-waterline marine perspective, ~40mm lens, coastal sea chop.
* **Lighting Environment:** Bright marine sunlight with realistic water surface reflections and spray.
* **Shadow Dynamics:** Hull shadow beneath overhang and wheelhouse brow.
* **Depth of Field & Sharpness:** Deep nautical focus on vessel superstructure.

## 2. Subjects & Uniform Compliance
* **Human Representation:** Vessel crew at distance.
* **Uniform & PPE Standard:** Navy marine workwear.

## 3. Brand Protection & Sanitization
* **Third-Party Marks Eliminated:** Removed 'FAST MAMMOTH' and foreign roundel emblem.
* **Official Branding Placements:** 'MEDDY GLOBAL SOLUTIONS LTD' in crisp charcoal capitals across cream superstructure; official logo roundel.

## 4. Safe Zone Validation
Primary corporate elements are positioned strictly within the safe viewport band (`y = 22%–58%`, `x = 8%–92%`), ensuring complete visibility without truncation when cropped by service cards or header gradients.
