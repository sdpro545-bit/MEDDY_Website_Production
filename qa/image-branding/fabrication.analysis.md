# Visual & Retouching Analysis: fabrication.webp

**Target Dimensions:** `900x1200 (3:4 aspect ratio)`  
**Site Crop Mode:** `3:4 service card; arc core and helmet branding positioned within primary vertical safe band.`

---

## 1. Photographic Properties
* **Camera Angle & Optics:** Close-up macro action shot, ~85mm lens, intense dynamic sparks.
* **Lighting Environment:** High-contrast welding arc core with brilliant specular highlights and deep surrounding shadows.
* **Shadow Dynamics:** Deep industrial contrast with dramatic silhouette lighting.
* **Depth of Field & Sharpness:** Tight depth of field focused on welding bead and gloved hands.

## 2. Subjects & Uniform Compliance
* **Human Representation:** Nigerian structural welder, deep brown hands/forearms with realistic specular highlights from arc.
* **Uniform & PPE Standard:** Blue coverall sleeve with charcoal cuff; charcoal leather welding glove; welding mask.

## 3. Brand Protection & Sanitization
* **Third-Party Marks Eliminated:** Eliminated any third-party manufacturer stamp on steel or helmet.
* **Official Branding Placements:** Vinyl logo decal on side of welding helmet; structural steel QA certification placard.

## 4. Safe Zone Validation
Primary corporate elements are positioned strictly within the safe viewport band (`y = 22%–58%`, `x = 8%–92%`), ensuring complete visibility without truncation when cropped by service cards or header gradients.
