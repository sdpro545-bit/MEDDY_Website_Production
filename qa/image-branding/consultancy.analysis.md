# Visual & Retouching Analysis: consultancy.webp

**Target Dimensions:** `900x1200 (3:4 aspect ratio)`  
**Site Crop Mode:** `Card 3:4 crop on service listing; middle 4:3 band on service detail. Title block protected within safe margin.`

---

## 1. Photographic Properties
* **Camera Angle & Optics:** Medium-close angled desktop shot, ~45mm focal length, clean architectural perspective.
* **Lighting Environment:** Warm studio drawing office lamp light from upper left, casting soft directional shadows across plan.
* **Shadow Dynamics:** Subtle pencil and ruler cast shadows across drawing board.
* **Depth of Field & Sharpness:** Shallow to medium DoF focused on drawing hands and technical schematic.

## 2. Subjects & Uniform Compliance
* **Human Representation:** Nigerian engineering consultant, deep brown hands and forearms with realistic knuckles and skin texture.
* **Uniform & PPE Standard:** Charcoal corporate polo with brand-blue collar band and embroidered logo-footer on left chest.

## 3. Brand Protection & Sanitization
* **Third-Party Marks Eliminated:** Removed foreign 'KRA' embroidered chest logo and third-party drafting markings.
* **Official Branding Placements:** Embroidered chest logo on charcoal polo; technical drawing title block in lower-right corner with 'MEDDY GLOBAL SOLUTIONS LTD' and logo.

## 4. Safe Zone Validation
Primary corporate elements are positioned strictly within the safe viewport band (`y = 22%–58%`, `x = 8%–92%`), ensuring complete visibility without truncation when cropped by service cards or header gradients.
