# Visual & Retouching Analysis: oem.webp

**Target Dimensions:** `900x1200 (3:4 aspect ratio)`  
**Site Crop Mode:** `3:4 service card; team and central placard centered in primary safe zone.`

---

## 1. Photographic Properties
* **Camera Angle & Optics:** Eye-level industrial workshop team shot, ~50mm lens.
* **Lighting Environment:** High-bay industrial facility lighting with balanced fill.
* **Shadow Dynamics:** Natural floor drop shadows and bench occlusion.
* **Depth of Field & Sharpness:** Medium depth of field capturing team and component workbench.

## 2. Subjects & Uniform Compliance
* **Human Representation:** Three Nigerian engineering professionals in uniform with natural hair and distinct features.
* **Uniform & PPE Standard:** Brand-blue coveralls with charcoal yokes and silver retro-reflective bands; white hard hats with logo.

## 3. Brand Protection & Sanitization
* **Third-Party Marks Eliminated:** Removed all foreign component crates and apparel marks.
* **Official Branding Placements:** Helmet logos; chest patches; precision OEM inspection placard on foreground equipment casing.

## 4. Safe Zone Validation
Primary corporate elements are positioned strictly within the safe viewport band (`y = 22%–58%`, `x = 8%–92%`), ensuring complete visibility without truncation when cropped by service cards or header gradients.
