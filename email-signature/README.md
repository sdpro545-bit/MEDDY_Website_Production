# MEDDY Official Webmail Signature Setup Guide (S1)

This guide explains how to install the official branded HTML email signature into **Namecheap Private Email** webmail interface.

---

## 1. Preparing Your Signature

1. Open `email-signature/signature.html` in any text editor or browser.
2. Replace the two placeholders with your actual details:
   - Replace `{{SENDER_NAME}}` with your full name (e.g., `Engr. Babatunde Lawal`).
   - Replace `{{SENDER_TITLE}}` with your official corporate role (e.g., `Managing Director` or `Lead Mechanical Engineer`).
3. Copy the entire HTML markup.

---

## 2. Installing in Namecheap Private Email Webmail

Follow these exact steps within Namecheap Private Email:

1. **Sign In:** Go to [https://privateemail.com](https://privateemail.com) and log in with your email (`info@meddyglobalsolutions.com`) and mailbox password.
2. **Access Settings:** Click the **Gear icon (Settings)** in the top-right corner of the webmail navigation bar.
3. **Navigate to Signatures:**
   - In the left sidebar, click **Mail**.
   - Under Mail options, click **Signatures**.
4. **Create Signature:**
   - Click **Add new signature** (or edit default signature).
   - Give the signature a name (e.g., `MEDDY Corporate Official`).
5. **Paste HTML:**
   - In the signature editor toolbar, click the **HTML Source** icon (`<>` or `HTML`).
   - Paste your customized HTML markup.
   - Click the HTML icon again to switch back to visual view to preview the layout.
6. **Set Default Rules:**
   - Check the box to use this signature for **New messages**.
   - Check the box to use this signature for **Replies and forwards**.
   - Set signature placement to: *Above quoted text*.
7. **Save:** Click **Save** at the bottom of the page.

---

## 3. Visual & Technical Verification

- Compose a test email to your personal address.
- Verify that:
  - The MEDDY logo renders cleanly on the left.
  - The phone, WhatsApp, email, and website links are clickable and direct to the verified destinations.
  - The signature maintains alignment on both mobile phone screens and desktop email clients.
